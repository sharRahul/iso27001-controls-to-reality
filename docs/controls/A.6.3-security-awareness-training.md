# A.6.3 Information Security Awareness, Education and Training

## Control objective

Ensure personnel receive appropriate information security awareness, education, and training so they understand their responsibilities and can recognise common threats.

## Practical implementation scope

Include:

- Employees.
- Contractors and temporary workers.
- Privileged administrators.
- Developers and technical teams.
- High-risk business functions such as finance, HR, and customer operations.

## Implementation steps

### 1. Define the training programme

Document:

- Mandatory annual security awareness training.
- Onboarding training for new starters.
- Role-based training for privileged users, developers, and control owners.
- Phishing simulation or social engineering awareness cadence.
- Policy acknowledgement requirements.
- Completion tracking and escalation for overdue training.

### 2. Map training to risk

Training should cover topics relevant to the organisation, such as:

- Phishing and credential theft.
- MFA fatigue and suspicious authentication prompts.
- Data handling and classification.
- Secure use of cloud services and collaboration platforms.
- Incident reporting.
- Acceptable use.
- Secure development and change control for technical teams.
- Privileged access responsibilities for administrators.

### 3. Review completion and effectiveness

Collect evidence showing:

- Training assignment list.
- Completion status.
- Overdue user escalation.
- Phishing simulation trends.
- Targeted remediation for repeated failures.
- Management review of awareness metrics.

## Audit questions

- Is security awareness mandatory for all in-scope personnel?
- Are new starters trained before or shortly after access is granted?
- How are overdue users escalated?
- Is role-based training provided for privileged users and developers?
- Are training results reviewed by management?
- Are phishing simulation outcomes used to improve awareness?

## Common gaps

- Training exists but contractors are excluded.
- Completion reports are not retained for the audit period.
- Phishing simulation failures are not followed up.
- Privileged users receive the same generic training as all users.
- Policy acknowledgement is not linked to the current policy version.
