## Common audit gaps

- SoA says a control is implemented but no evidence exists.
- Evidence is a screenshot without date, system name, or scope.
- Control owner cannot explain how the control operates.
- Exceptions exist informally but are not risk accepted.
- Evidence is collected only once and not refreshed.
- Technical settings exist but are not connected to policy, risk, or monitoring.
- Access reviews are performed but remediation is not tracked.
